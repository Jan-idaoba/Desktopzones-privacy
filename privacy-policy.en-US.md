# DesktopZones Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

## Overview

DesktopZones (the "App") is a window zone management utility. We value your
privacy: the App **does not collect, store, or transmit any personally
identifiable information**, and it does not connect to any network service.

## Data We Process

The App processes only the technical data necessary for its core features,
entirely on your device, and none of it leaves your device:

- Window titles, positions, and sizes — used transiently in memory only for
  zone snapping and thumbnail previews, and not persisted;
- Layout configuration and UI language — stored in your local app-data
  directory / registry;
- Runtime logs and crash dumps — written only to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\DesktopZones`
  on your device, for troubleshooting.

## Data Sharing

We do not share data. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising,
or tracking components, and it does not send the data above to us or to any
third party.

## Data Storage and Security

All data is stored only on your local device and protected by operating-system
permissions. Crash dumps may contain a snapshot of part of the App's process
memory (technical data) and are used only for troubleshooting.

## Your Rights

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is no personal data to
access, correct, or delete. To fully remove data the App has created locally,
uninstall the App and delete the local directories mentioned above.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data
from anyone.

## Policy Updates

If future changes alter how data is processed, we will update this policy on
this page.
